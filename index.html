Our Story

A quiet corner of Nakuru, made for rest

Silver Side Hotel began with a simple idea: that a stay should feel like being looked after. From the first greeting to the last cup of coffee, every detail is shaped around comfort, calm and genuine Kenyan hospitality.

Whether you are here for business, a weekend away or a family celebration, our team is ready to make your time with us effortless.

Can I host a meeting or private event?

Yes. We offer meeting space and private dining. Message us your date and group size for options and rates.
