Open index.html to start. Pages: index, rooms, dining, about, privacy, cookies, terms.

BEFORE YOU GO LIVE (needed for Google):
1. Replace https://silversideehotel.vercel.app with your real website address in ALL .html files,
   sitemap.xml and robots.txt. (Use find-and-replace across the folder.)
2. Add the site to Google Search Console and submit https://silversideehotel.vercel.app/sitemap.xml
3. Claim/verify the hotel's Google Business Profile with the same phone number (0746 816 008).
