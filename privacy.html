Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Silver Side Hotel (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, how we use it and the choices you have, in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Information we collect

  • Details you give us in the booking, reservation and table forms: name, phone number, dates, room type, number of guests and any message.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp or by phone.
  • Basic technical information such as device type and pages visited, if you accept cookies.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries and confirm bookings and table reservations.
  • To prepare for your stay and provide our services.
  • To improve our website and services.

How forms work

Our forms do not store your details on this website. When you submit one, it opens WhatsApp with your message pre-filled, and you choose whether to send it. Messages sent on WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with staff who need it to serve you, with service providers acting for us, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep booking details only as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal and accounting obligations.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.
